## 🎬 1. Automated Video & Shorts Production

### [wide-trace/open-higgsfield](https://github.com/wide-trace/open-higgsfield.git)
- **What It Does:** Open-source Next.js 16 + React 19 generative studio that drives 38 AI foundation models (Kling 3, Wan, Flux, Seedance 2.5, Ideogram, Soul Cinema) from a single unified composer.
- **Business Growth Playbook:**
  - **High-Converting Video Ad Hooks:** Generate cinema-quality video hooks, product mockups, and dynamic video b-roll without paying $50–$100/mo for Midjourney or Runway/Higgsfield studio subscriptions.
  - **Recreate & Batch Variants:** Use the built-in Recreate workflow to iterate on winning ad visuals, testing 4 aspect ratios and batch variants per prompt across multiple video models simultaneously.

### [Comfy-Org/ComfyUI](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI.git)
- **What It Does:** Modular, graph/DAG-based execution engine and UI for Stable Diffusion, FLUX, SDXL, Wan2.1, LTX-Video, and ControlNet. Features VRAM tensor caching, headless REST API, and WebSocket event queues.
- **Business Growth Playbook:**
  - **Self-Hosted Ad Creative Factory:** Execute FLUX.1 + ControlNet workflows to generate pixel-perfect marketing images with exact product placement and brand typography, completely eliminating third-party SaaS credit costs.
  - **Automated Video B-Roll Generation:** Connect Wan2.1 or LTX-Video ComfyUI workflows directly to headless marketing scripts to churn out continuous background video clips for social media ads and product reels.

### [harry0703/MoneyPrinterTurbo](https://github.com/harry0703/MoneyPrinterTurbo.git)
- **What It Does:** Full end-to-end automated short-video generator. Input a keyword or script, and it writes the story, downloads relevant stock B-roll (Pexels), synthesizes voiceovers (Edge-TTS), synchronizes animated word-by-word subtitles (Whisper), adds background music, and exports a 9:16 vertical MP4 video.
- **Business Growth Playbook:**
  - **Batch Video Factory:** Generate 5 daily vertical short videos on trending industry topics to publish on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
  - **Headless Microservice:** Deploy via Docker Compose (`:8080`) to automate programmatic video creation triggered directly by blog posts or new product releases.

### [latent-spaces/brag](https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag.git)
- **What It Does:** Agent skill that analyzes a GitHub repository or product URL, extracts the core value proposition, writes a 4-part punchy script, and renders a 20-second 60FPS motion graphic video with sound effects and multi-platform social launch copy (`share_copy.md`).
- **Business Growth Playbook:**
  - **Product Feature Launches:** Run `/brag` whenever you ship a new feature or version to immediately produce high-production launch collateral for Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and Product Hunt without spending hours in video editors.

### [CristianOlivera1/openvid](https://github.com/CristianOlivera1/openvid.git)
- **What It Does:** Free in-browser video editor that turns raw screen recordings into professional product demo videos with 3D perspective camera tilts, smooth keyframe zooms, background gradients, and sleek browser frames (Arc, Safari, Chrome). 100% client-side via WebAssembly.
- **Business Growth Playbook:**
  - **High-Converting Product Walkthroughs:** Record software features, apply cinematic 3D zooms to highlight buttons, and export 4K demo videos for your landing page hero and social announcement feeds.

### [ethanplusai/harvey](https://github.com/ethanplusai/harvey.git)
- **What It Does:** Autonomous outbound B2B sales agent running locally on your existing Claude Pro/Max (via Claude Code CLI) or ChatGPT Plus/Team (via Codex CLI) subscription with **$0 model API bill**.
- **Business Growth Playbook:**
  - **Zero-Cost Local Lead Discovery:** Automatically discovers prospective businesses in any city/niche via OpenStreetMap for $0, without paying monthly fees for Apollo, ZoomInfo, or Clay.
  - **23-Signal Tactical Cold Outreach:** Inspects prospective client websites (via 3 free HTTP requests) to find high-intent buying signals: active Google/Meta ad spend, incumbent agencies credited in footers ("Website by", "Powered by"), missing schema markup, or no online booking.
  - **Anti-Slop Copywriting & LAARC Inbound Negotiation:** Generates 3-email cold outreach sequences (<75 words, strictly banning generic AI clichés) and autonomously handles inbound email objections (Listen $\rightarrow$ Acknowledge $\rightarrow$ Assess $\rightarrow$ Respond $\rightarrow$ Confirm) to book discovery calls.

- **Business Growth Playbook:**
  - **High-Budget B2B Intent Enrichment:** Active hiring in engineering or marketing is the single highest-converting B2B buying signal. Feed domains with open requisitions directly into `ethanplusai/harvey` to trigger context-aware outreach referencing their active team expansion.
  - **Zero-Cost Talent & Creator Sourcing:** Query live tech stack requirements, market salaries, and open developer/creator roles via the FreeHire MCP server inside Claude Code or Cursor without paying for LinkedIn Recruiter licenses.
